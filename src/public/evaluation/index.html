Three layers of checks

Each layer checks the one above it. The AI never produces a number on its own: it reads the simulation and picks.

What this does not prove

  • The picks are scored on the card values the AI reads (default settings, 10 runs); the 10-run check after a pick is not repeated in the benchmark.
  • "Best" is our written rule per CEO goal. Another reasonable rule could rank near ties differently.
  • The number check confirms that each number appears in what the AI was given, not that it is used in the right sentence.
  • In Machinery breakdown the effects are small against the run-to-run noise, so at 10 runs no countermeasure is clearly effective and the AI correctly answers "none".
  • The benchmark measures the countermeasure pick (gpt-4o). The explanation chat runs on Claude Opus 4.8; its answers show a number check in the page.
